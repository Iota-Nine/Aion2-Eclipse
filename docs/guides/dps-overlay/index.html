Read DPS and total damage together

DPS is a damage rate. Total damage is the amount recorded for the encounter. Read both when reviewing a player’s contribution: a moment without damage is different from losing the cumulative total.

The HUD shows group statistics and individual rows. Class icons identify received class information; larger received rosters use scrolling. Healing and selected support events have their own presentation.

Choose your layout

Use compact mode for the essentials or full mode for more detail. Fold the HUD to its header when you want more space, then expand it again. You can move the overlay, while the full client remains useful for reviewing a run on another screen.

Sharing only the AION 2 window in a streaming app may omit Eclipse’s separate window. If you need both in your stream, select a capture source that includes both windows.

Four useful shortcuts

These are the default bindings. Your startup guide and Preferences show availability and any saved changes.

  1. Alt+F8: show or hide the HUD.
  2. Ctrl+Alt+L: lock or unlock mouse interaction.
  3. Alt+R: keep the current run as interrupted and reset the counter.
  4. Alt+M: stop the current alarms.

Keep the encounter readable

Use total damage and saved history for a full-run review. Internal teleports and tested resurrection identity changes preserve the current run’s totals. Use a manual reset when you deliberately want to begin a new measurement.
