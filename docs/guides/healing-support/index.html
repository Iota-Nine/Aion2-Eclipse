Look beyond damage

The HUD can show healing alongside the damage rows. Party analysis adds detail about received healing and supported events. Comparing these contributions helps you review what a support character did during the run.

Received healing is not the same as effective healing. The meter does not know overheal, so a larger received value alone is not a complete measure of a healer’s performance.

What shields show

For selected supported shield skills, Eclipse records observed applications, recipients and received durations when attribution is reliable. Shield support covers selected skills across multiple classes; it is not limited to Cleric.

A received duration is not actual uptime. Shield HP, absorbed damage and the moment a shield breaks are unknown. The meter does not invent a protection amount when the event does not provide one.

Cleric and Chanter support

Selected beneficial buff families from Cleric and Chanter can be attributed when caster and party recipient are identified. Repeated mantra refreshes are grouped into an observed sequence.

These observations do not calculate the buff’s power or the extra damage it caused. Ambiguous events and unsupported skills do not become credited support statistics.

Review a specific run

Open a saved dungeon run in party analysis and compare damage, received healing and supported effects in that same encounter. Optional self-support detail is available separately; check Preferences for your saved presentation choices.
