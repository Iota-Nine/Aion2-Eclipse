Open a run from history

Select a completed or interrupted run in history to open its party analysis. Review each player’s total damage, the damage bars, skills, critical hits and combat timeline. Go back to history to choose another run.

Notes and favourites help you keep useful encounters. The archives are stored on your PC; a website account is not required to read them.

Keep dungeon totals together

Internal teleports retain the current run. Recognised final-boss deaths archive supported dungeons, while other finishes remain manual. A departure before completion can be kept as an interrupted run.

Version 3.0.15 also preserves the current run when a local session ID is reused before resurrection metadata arrives. Alt+R deliberately keeps the current run as interrupted before starting a fresh counter.

Compare your own builds

Save personal build presets and compare your recorded results. Pick encounters with similar targets, duration and party conditions to make the comparison useful.

A change in damage may come from the encounter or your group as well as your build. Eclipse’s comparisons use your own archives; they do not represent a live global meta ranking.

Give support its context

Review received healing and selected shield or buff observations alongside damage where available. A support player’s contribution deserves context, and unknown measurements remain unknown rather than becoming guessed values.

ECLIPSE GUIDES

Make every tool yours.

Setup, shortcuts and clearer combat results. Practical guides for your next AION 2 run.

01

Install Eclipse

Set up Eclipse on Windows 10 or 11, install Npcap when needed, see your first AION 2 combat stats and learn the overlay shortcuts.

02

DPS overlay & shortcuts

Use Eclipse’s compact AION 2 overlay for party DPS, character damage and support. Learn Alt+F8, mouse locking, run reset and alarm controls.

03

Boss timers & alerts

Track AION 2 bosses, Rifts and favourites in Eclipse. Set 10-minute, 5-minute or scheduled-time reminders and stop alarms with Alt+M.

04

Healing & support

Understand Eclipse’s AION 2 healing and support stats: received healing, observed shield uses and selected Cleric and Chanter buffs, alongside party damage.

05

Dungeon history & analysis

Review AION 2 dungeon runs in Eclipse: each player’s total damage, skills, critical hits and support, with local history and personal build comparisons.
