A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

Before you jump in.

Report a bug
Do I need an account to use the meter?+

No. Combat tools and local run history work without a website account. An account is for sending private suggestions and following replies.

How does Eclipse read combat?+

Eclipse reads game-network events received on your PC using Npcap. Capture depends on the protocol and traffic; missing data stays unknown. A game update can require an Eclipse update.

What do boss timers show?+

EU field-boss timers use times received from the game’s field-monster map for your detected server. Open that map to synchronise. Regional calendar cards are forecasts with a separate clock confirmation; they do not prove a boss is alive.

When does a dungeon reset?+

Internal teleports retain the run. Recognised final-boss deaths archive supported dungeons; interrupted runs are retained. Other finishes remain manual. Alt+R keeps the interrupted run before resetting.

What is measured for healers and shields?+

Received healing and selected support events can be shown. Shields use observed applications and received durations. Shield HP, absorption, destruction, actual uptime, effective healing and overheal are not measured.

Where is my data stored?+

Combat archives and client settings stay on your PC. The site stores only reports and private suggestion conversations you submit, with accounts handled by the identity provider.
